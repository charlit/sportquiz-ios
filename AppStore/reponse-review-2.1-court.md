1. Screen recording
It starts by launching the app from the Home Screen and shows: a "Spécial Foot" game, a "Hardcore" game (one life, choice of country), sharing a score, the "Aventure" mode (map of French clubs, stages, countries unlocked one by one), the leaderboards and the native Game Center screen, the badges, the settings and the contact button. The app has no account, login, user-generated content, paid content or in-app purchases.

2. Purpose and audience
Football Quiz is a free French-language football trivia game for French-speaking fans of all ages: more than 4,000 multiple-choice questions on club history, the World Cup and the Africa Cup of Nations, with increasing difficulty, a timer, points, badges and Game Center leaderboards. No ads.

3. Instructions
No login or setup needed; all modes are playable from the home screen:
- Spécial Foot: 100 questions, 3 lives.
- Hardcore: choose "Tout le foot" or a country; one wrong answer ends the game.
- Aventure: France, then a city on the map and a club; 10 stages per club (4/5 correct answers unlock the next). Finishing all French clubs unlocks the next country of your choice.
- Classement: leaderboards per mode; "Ouvrir dans Game Center" opens Game Center.
- Profile (top right): badges, contact, vibration setting.
Game Center sign-in is optional; without it everything works except leaderboards.

4. External services
- Apple Game Center for leaderboards.
- GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com): the app downloads updated question files (JSON data only, no code) from the public repository github.com/charlit/sportquiz. Nothing is sent; if the download fails, the bundled questions are used.
No analytics, ads, authentication, payment or AI services. No personal data is collected.

5. Regions
French content only, distributed in French-speaking countries. Features and content are identical in every region.

6. Third-party material
Not a regulated industry. All questions are original factual trivia written by the developer. Clubs, players and competitions are only named as facts; no logos, crests, official photos, videos or branding; flags are simple drawings. The app is not affiliated with any club, league or federation.
