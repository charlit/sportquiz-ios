The attached video starts with launching the app and shows the typical flow: the home screen, a "Spécial Foot" game (100 questions, 3 lives), a "Hardcore" game (one life, choice of country), the "Aventure" mode (map of French clubs, stages to unlock, countries unlocked one by one), the leaderboards with the native Game Center screen, the badges, the profile with the settings (vibrations), and sharing a score. The app has no account registration, no login, no user-generated content and no paid content or in-app purchases.

2. Purpose and target audience
Football Quiz is a French-language football trivia game for French-speaking football fans of all ages. It offers more than 4,000 multiple-choice questions on club history, the FIFA World Cup and the Africa Cup of Nations, with increasing difficulty, a timer and points. Players test and improve their football knowledge, collect badges and compare scores with other players through Game Center leaderboards. The app is free, with no ads.

3. Instructions
No login or setup is required. Every mode is playable directly from the home screen:
- "Spécial Foot": 100 questions, 3 lives.
- "Hardcore": choose "Tout le foot" or a country; one wrong answer ends the game.
- "Aventure": pick France, then a city on the map and a club; each club has 10 stages (4 correct answers out of 5 unlock the next one). Completing all French clubs unlocks the choice of the next country.
- "Classement": leaderboards per mode; the "Ouvrir dans Game Center" button opens the native Game Center screen.
- Profile (top right): badges, contact button, vibration setting.
Game Center sign-in is optional; without it the game works fully, only the leaderboards are unavailable.

4. External services
- Apple Game Center: leaderboards (scores and player names are handled by Apple).
- GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com): the app downloads updated question files (JSON data only, no executable code) from the public repository github.com/charlit/sportquiz so that new questions can be added without an app update. Nothing is sent; if the download fails, the questions bundled in the app are used.
No analytics, advertising, authentication, payment or AI services are used. The app collects no personal data.

5. Regional differences
The app's content is in French only and it is distributed in French-speaking countries. Features and content are identical in every region.

6. Third-party material and regulation
The app does not operate in a regulated industry. All questions are original factual trivia written by the developer. Clubs, players and competitions are only mentioned by name as facts; the app contains no logos, crests, official photos, videos or official branding, and flags are simple drawings made in the app. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any club, league or federation.
